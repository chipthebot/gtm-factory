# Mission - B2B SaaS Launch Demos

## Mission

Find early-stage B2B SaaS companies with recent public launch signals and a
visible gap in demo, storyboard, or founder-led launch content.

## Thesis

SaaS teams need clear demos, launch posts, social cards, changelog visuals,
and product explainers. Public launch pages and founder posts often reveal the
product story well enough to write a useful demo-direction packet.

## Fit Signals

- Seed or Series A stage if public.
- Recent launch, changelog, funding, Product Hunt post, founder post, or
  hiring signal.
- Clear buyer and product promise.
- Public product, pricing, demo, or launch page.
- Visual demo story is thin or hard to understand.

## Reject Fast

- Mature company with an obvious marketing team.
- Product cannot be understood without login.
- Security, compliance, or regulated claims dominate the pitch.
- Public context is too shallow.

## Packet Output

Include:

- public product story
- recent signal
- buyer and use case
- demo clarity audit
- 3 demo or launch-story directions
- 1 product video/GIF storyboard
- founder/social hooks
- risks and claims to verify
