# Mission - Local Service Creative Audit

## Mission

Find local service businesses in one metro where public brand material suggests
a clear creative, trust, or lead-generation gap.

## Thesis

Local service businesses often have valuable proof: job photos, reviews,
service pages, neighborhoods, certifications, and before/after work. Many do
not package that proof well. A small public creative audit can show the gap.

## Fit Signals

- Local operator with a clear service area.
- Enough reviews to suggest an active business.
- Public website or business profile.
- Job photos, portfolio, service pages, or social posts are visible.
- Marketing looks dated, inconsistent, or underused.

## Reject Fast

- National chain or franchise with centralized marketing.
- Too few public reviews to judge.
- No public website or business profile.
- Already polished across site, ads, photos, and social.
- Personal, medical, legal, or emergency details create privacy risk.

## Packet Output

Include:

- public source summary
- trust and proof inventory
- current creative audit
- 3 ad or content directions
- 1 short-form concept
- 5 headline hooks
- rights and privacy cautions
