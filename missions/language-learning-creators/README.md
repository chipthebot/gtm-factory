# Mission - Language Learning Creators

## Mission

Find language-learning creators with public learning resources that could be
turned into a finished study deck, companion pack, or repeat-review product.

No outreach is sent from this repo.

## Thesis

Language creators often already sell or give away structured material:
courses, transcripts, vocabulary lists, worksheets, books, memberships,
podcasts, and flashcard packs. That gives the mission a concrete first
deliverable: show how one public lesson or resource could become a polished
study artifact.

## Fit Signals

- Clear language-learning audience.
- Public course, lesson, transcript, worksheet, vocabulary list, book, or deck.
- Creator-owned site, newsletter, shop, membership, or public profile.
- Audience likely values repeated practice.
- Stable public dedupe key.

## Reject Fast

- No learner-facing resource.
- Mostly entertainment with no study surface.
- No stable profile, site, or public source.
- Claims around immigration, exams, medical, legal, or financial outcomes need
  careful human review.
- The useful artifact would require paid or private material.

## Packet Output

For priority packets, include:

- public source summary
- audience and resource fit
- one study-deck or companion-pack concept
- permissions needed before using content
- risk flags
- optional drafts for human review

Default to source maps instead of storing screenshots, lesson text, audio,
PDFs, or paid material.
