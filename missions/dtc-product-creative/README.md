# Mission - DTC Product Creative

## Mission

Find DTC brands where public product pages, reviews, ads, and social posts
show a clear opportunity for fresher product creative.

## Thesis

DTC teams already need ad hooks, creator briefs, landing-page angles, product
shots, and offer tests. Public pages often expose enough signal to write a
useful creative packet before any sales conversation.

## Fit Signals

- Clear hero product.
- Public product page, reviews, social profile, and ad examples.
- Active paid or organic creative pressure.
- Visible offer, bundle, launch, sale, or new SKU.
- Creative variety looks thin or stale.

## Reject Fast

- Mega-brand or clearly polished creative machine.
- No public product page.
- No useful social, review, or ad surface.
- Regulated claims are too sensitive for a cold creative test.

## Packet Output

Include:

- public source summary
- product and offer notes
- review themes as short paraphrases
- current creative audit
- 3 ad-angle directions
- 1 creator-style script or storyboard
- risks and claims to verify
