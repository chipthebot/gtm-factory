# Mission - YouTube Educator Packaging

## Mission

Find educational YouTube channels with useful content and visible packaging
gaps: thumbnails, titles, shorts, playlists, or newsletter bridges.

## Thesis

Educational creators publish public material by default. Many have strong
substance but inconsistent packaging. A useful first deliverable is a small
thumbnail/title and short-form direction pack tied to one recent video.

## Fit Signals

- 10+ meaningful videos.
- Clear educational topic.
- Recent uploads.
- Mixed view patterns or inconsistent thumbnails.
- Public contact route, site, or newsletter.

## Reject Fast

- Celebrity-scale creator with a professional team.
- No recent uploads.
- No stable channel URL or contact route.
- Topic is too sensitive for cold packaging advice.

## Packet Output

Include:

- recent video context
- thumbnail/title audit
- 5 thumbnail-title directions
- 3 short-form ideas
- one channel-packaging note
- risks and claims to verify
