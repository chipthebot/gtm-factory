# Prompt - YouTube Educator Packaging

Fill placeholders:

- `{{BATCH_LABEL}}`
- `{{BATCH_SLUG}}`
- `{{COUNT}}`
- `{{FOCUS}}`

## Mission

For `{{COUNT}}` YouTube educators in `{{FOCUS}}`, build public research
packets with packaging directions for human review.

Do not contact anyone. Do not download full videos or generate media.

## Steps

1. Find creators through YouTube search, related channels, public newsletters,
   creator websites, X, LinkedIn, Instagram, and TikTok.

2. Disqualify fast:
   - too large or clearly professionally packaged
   - fewer than 10 meaningful videos
   - no recent uploads
   - sensitive advice dominates the channel

3. Collect:
   - channel name and URL
   - website or newsletter
   - topic and subscriber band if public
   - recent video URLs
   - thumbnail/title patterns
   - shorts strategy
   - time-sensitive signal

4. Save source assets when appropriate:
   - `channel-home.png`
   - `recent-thumbnails/`
   - `recent-titles.txt`
   - `recent-video-pages/`
   - `shorts-grid.png`
   - `website-or-newsletter.png`
   - `colors.txt`
   - `fonts.txt`

5. Audit 1 to 5:
   - thumbnail clarity
   - title specificity
   - first-30-second promise
   - shorts repurposing
   - visual identity
   - newsletter/product bridge
   - topic differentiation

6. Write `packet.md` with 5 thumbnail/title directions for one recent video,
   3 shorts ideas, 1 channel-packaging note, diagnosis, and risks.

7. Append a row to `leads.{{BATCH_SLUG}}.csv` and update
   `prospect-index.local.csv`.

## Done

- Packets tie recommendations to public videos.
- Assets are research inputs only.
- No outreach was sent.
